## What 100% means

**100% means all explicit acceptance gates for the approved, bounded, playable Dinosaur Horror RPG product have passed**, not an assertion that all possible dinosaur encounters, species, settings, future stories or generated campaigns have been exhausted. It is **not** a percent of time worked, test coverage, engineering effort, money spent or a probability of finishing on any particular date. An accepted gate earns its full planning weight only with source-backed evidence and verification; **partial means real work exists but earns zero points until the entire gate passes**. Never infer implementation from an architecture document alone.

The core vision remains **a first-person/party natural-language dinosaur survival-horror roleplaying experience**, with *Alien RPG Evolved Edition* mechanics adjudicated behind the player interface and original setting/scenario material. Explicit user requirements remain permanent:

- **The deterministic Evolved game engine is independent of any one adventure.** Scenario definitions, cast, motives, secrets, dinosaurs, physical places, valid encounters, evolving objectives and narrative material are all replaceable, validated content—not special cases for *After the Storm*.
- **Party, Focus and Spectator** support free switching. Any/all characters can be hand-created, auto-generated or hybrid customized. The player sees playable character sheets but need not see dice/math during actual play. Use established Year Zero/Evolved rules, with documented original dinosaur adaptations and real dice behind the screen.
- **Several different authored adventures**, each with meaningful seeded replay variability, plus an **open-ended generator** for fresh Cinematic stories and continuing Campaign chapters. A generated storyline may use one to five acts, or optional linked scenes, with real alternate outcomes; **no mandatory three acts, attack or rescue template**. Generation must not retroactively rewrite earned facts.
- **The three linked dramatic pillars: dinosaur horror/suspense, action and sense of wonder.** Dinosaurs are animals with credible motives, not obligatory monsters or friendly park attractions. Normalcy, awe, nature, interpersonal conflict, threat, retreat and recovery should alternate causally, not according to forced tension ramps. A full island wildlife population sim is not required.
- **The AI Game Mother is a creative participant but never the physical/rules authority.** It understands natural language, plays NPCs, frames scenes and proposes permitted exceptional effects. Engine-verified physics, awareness, equipment, action budgets and committed dice outcomes determine truth. No unearned knowledge, arbitrary mechanical patches, fabricated rolls or client-held confidential GM facts presented as truly secure.
- **Compatibility and quality are acceptance criteria.** Freeze historical scenario fingerprints/saves; test all modes and meaningful variations on desktop/mobile; protect Worker secrets and future GM state.

**Authoritative contracts:** [Evolved Rules](EVOLVED-RULES-AUTHORITY.md) · [Adventure/Engine Separation](ADVENTURE-ENGINE-SEPARATION.md) · [Procedural Replayability](PROCEDURAL-ADVENTURES-REPLAYABILITY.md) · [Character Generation](CHARACTER-GENERATION-MODES.md) · [Game Mother](ARCHITECTURE-AI-GM.md) · [Wonder and Dread](WONDER-AND-DREAD-DESIGN.md) · [Living Implementation Plan](DINOSAUR-EVOLVED-IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md). ## Development sequence after the roadmap audit

**Phase A — Mechanically credible survivors and NPCs (next).** Start from immutable 009-C3: source-bound nearby NPC perception, grounded threat reactions, Evolved wounds/turns, resource-constrained survivor attacks and associated creature results. Finish additional Evolved parity and connect the existing manual/random/hybrid character lab to actual roster play, relationships and later advancement. Keep dice and math behind the screen while preserving authoritative checks.

**Phase B — Finish the *game engine*, not one adventure.** Remove Nublar-exclusive Party/runtime branching without breaking historical `The Long Count` revisions 1–11, Nublar saves, varied seeds or group modes. Make all authored scenarios share interchangeable physical/rules systems and predictable save migration/recovery. Add several distinct, varied, original curated adventures and test replay differences beyond cosmetic variant flags.

**Phase C — Creative, source-grounded GM and generation.** Grow the bounded Game Mother into genuinely adaptable interpretation, exception adjudication, NPC conversation and cause-respecting narration. Generate full original Cinematic packages, then continuing chapters that persist injuries, relationships, meaningful outcomes and established world facts; design deliberately for terror/action/**wonder**, nonviolent exits and tension relief. The producer of scenario content must not be allowed to bypass the core compiler or change established state.

**Phase D — Privacy, reliability and release quality.** Move actual GM secrets to a trusted authority, validate save/restore, Worker access and unsafe model proposals, and complete hands-on desktop/mobile QA for Party, Focus and Spectator, authored variations, combat, camp/survey routes, NPCs, survival outcomes, character creation and ongoing chapters.

These phases are **dependencies and priorities**, not a strict rule that independent quality improvements or content ideas must wait for every earlier item. Do not start a giant island wildlife simulator to claim NPC realism.
